03 / Good to know

BEFORE THE
FIRST SIP.

Do I need to book ahead?+

Yes. We run small groups and one brewer leads every tour, so slots are limited. Write to us at least three days ahead.

What is the age limit?+

Tastings are 18 and over. Younger visitors are welcome on the tour without the tasting, with an adult.

How do I get there and where do I park?+

Industriska 12, Skopje. There is free parking at the entrance and a bus stop 300 metres away.

Is the space accessible?+

The brewery and the toilet are on the ground floor, no stairs. Tell us in advance so we can clear the route past the kettles.

What is in the beers?+

All five contain barley, and Magla also contains wheat — so gluten. We do not fine the beer with anything of animal origin.

Can I buy bottles to take home?+

Yes, on site after the tour. We do not sell online.
