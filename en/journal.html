01 / 18 June 2026

Why Kaluǵer Finishes Dry

A tripel is strong, but it should not feel heavy. Candi sugar helps create a dry, elegant finish.

Read the story +

Kaluǵer uses pilsner malt and Belgian candi sugar. The sugar lifts the strength without weighing down the body. Belgian yeast brings fruit and spice, while Styrian Goldings and Saaz balance the finish with 30 IBU.

Meet the beer

02 / 10 June 2026

Magla: Yeast Makes the Aroma

Banana and clove are not syrup. They are fermentation esters and phenols from wheat yeast.

Read the story +

Magla begins with a high proportion of wheat malt. Warm fermentation with weizen yeast creates its recognizable banana and clove aromas. Just 12 IBU of Tettnang gives the yeast room to tell the story.

Meet the beer

03 / 28 May 2026

Five Bottles, One Tasting Line

The order from Zrno to Koren is deliberate: each bottle reveals another side of the collection.

Read the story +

Start with crisp Zrno, then move to soft Magla. Kaluǵer brings a golden, dry, spicy character, Opat adds dark fruit and caramel, and Koren finishes with roasted malt. This is not a ladder of bitterness: each bottle opens up a different part of the collection.
