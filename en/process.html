01

Malt & milling

Pilsner and Vienna malt, finely milled for a clean, bready-sweet extract with no roast.

02

Mash

A single, mid-temperature mash for a well-attenuated, drinkable body.

03

Boil & hops

Noble Saaz and Hallertau added gently — just 18 IBU, enough to dry the finish.

04

Fermentation

Cold lager fermentation and a long rest for clean, ester-free clarity.

05

Conditioning & serving

Lagered until bright pale gold; served at 5–7°C in a tall glass.
